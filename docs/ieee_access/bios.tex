\begin{IEEEbiographynophoto}{Aksultan Mukhanbet}
received the bachelor's, M.S., and Ph.D. degrees from Al-Farabi Kazakh National
University, Almaty, Kazakhstan. He is currently a Lecturer with the Department
of Computer Science, Al-Farabi Kazakh National University. His teaching and
research activities cover database design, relational and NoSQL systems, data
integrity, machine learning, computer vision, high-performance computing,
software engineering, and quantum computing. He has contributed to research on
quantum algorithms, hybrid quantum--classical learning, intelligent video
surveillance, 5G network analytics, and industrial machine-learning
applications. He has authored and coauthored journal articles and conference
papers in these areas.
\end{IEEEbiographynophoto}

\begin{IEEEbiographynophoto}{Almaz Sapargali}
received the B.Sc. degree in cyberphysical systems from the International
Information Technology University (IITU), Almaty, Kazakhstan, in 2023. He is
currently pursuing the M.Sc. degree in computer science and technology with
Al-Farabi Kazakh National University, Almaty, Kazakhstan, under a double-degree
program with Northwestern Polytechnical University (NPU), China. His research
interests include deep reinforcement learning and scientific machine learning,
with a focus on surrogate-based control of building energy systems. Earlier
results from the present study were presented at the WCCM--ECCOMAS Congress
2026, Munich, Germany.
\end{IEEEbiographynophoto}

\begin{IEEEbiographynophoto}{Beimbet Daribayev}
received the B.S., M.S., and Ph.D. degrees in computer science from Al-Farabi
Kazakh National University, Almaty, Kazakhstan, in 2009, 2011, and 2014,
respectively. He is currently the Vice-Rector for Academic Affairs and a Member
of the Management Board of Shakarim University, Semey, Kazakhstan. His previous
positions include Software Engineer, Researcher, Associate Professor, Head of
the Computer Science Laboratory, and Head of the Department of Computer
Science. He has authored more than 50 scientific publications. His research
interests include high-performance computing, numerical modeling, machine
learning, quantum computing, and quantum--classical algorithms. He received the
Best University Teacher Award in 2019 and the State Scientific Scholarship for
Talented Young Scientists in 2022.
\end{IEEEbiographynophoto}

\begin{IEEEbiographynophoto}{Shona Shinassylov}
received the B.Sc. degree in machine building from the Kazakh National Technical
University, Almaty, Kazakhstan, in 2009, the M.Sc. degree in information
technology from the T. Ryskulov Kazakh Economic University, Almaty, in 2014,
and a second M.Sc. degree in the analysis of supercomputing and cloud
technological processes from the Faculty of Information Technologies, Al-Farabi
Kazakh National University, Almaty, in 2018, with a thesis on visualization
tools for oil-displacement computations on supercomputing systems. He completed
the doctoral program in system engineering at the same faculty in 2025. He is
currently with LLP Digit Alem and Al-Farabi Kazakh National University, Almaty,
where he takes part in scientific and innovation projects. His research
interests include information technology, artificial intelligence, and digital
twins.
\end{IEEEbiographynophoto}

\begin{IEEEbiographynophoto}{Paulo Trigo}
received the degree in applied mathematics from the Faculty of Sciences,
University of Lisbon, Lisbon, Portugal, in 1988, the M.S. degree in electrical
and computer engineering from Instituto Superior T\'ecnico, University of
Lisbon, in 1993, and the Ph.D. degree in informatics engineering and artificial
intelligence from the Faculty of Sciences, University of Lisbon, in 2007. He is
currently an Adjunct Professor with the Department of Computer Engineering,
Instituto Superior de Engenharia de Lisboa, and a Researcher with the
University of Lisbon. His research interests include artificial intelligence,
multi-agent systems, decision-making, machine learning, reinforcement learning,
knowledge representation, ontologies, and geographic information systems.
\end{IEEEbiographynophoto}
