# Author biographies — ASEJ submission

The submission portal has an **Author biography** slot. This is a portal requirement, not a rule in
the guide for authors, so there is no stated word limit; the Elsevier convention for engineering
journals is **~80–120 words per author**, third person, present tense.

**These are skeletons, not text you can submit as is.** Everything in `<<angle brackets>>` is a fact
only the author knows — degree, year, institution where it was obtained, current position, research
interests, publication record. Do not let anyone guess these: a wrong degree or affiliation in a
published biography needs a corrigendum.

What is already filled in below comes from the title page and the CRediT statement, so it is safe.

**These files must never be merged into `manuscript.tex`** — they identify the authors and the
review is double anonymized. They go into their own portal slot, like the title page.

## Author photos

The portal has a separate **Author photo** slot. Requirements follow the journal's general artwork
rules, since no separate photo spec is published:

- one file per author, ideally named by surname (`Mukhanbet.jpg`, `Sapargali.jpg`, …);
- JPEG or TIFF, **minimum 300 dpi** at the printed size (the artwork rule for halftones);
- head-and-shoulders, plain background, portrait orientation — a passport-style photo works;
- the author must own the rights to the photo, since it will be published under CC BY-NC-ND.

Nothing here can be produced without the authors: six photographs have to be collected before the
submission can be completed.
