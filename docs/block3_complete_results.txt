§1.  Methodology, pre-registration, and reading order
§1.1  Reading order
Block 1 established the surrogate-level claim: the v3 control-oriented surrogate and the calibrated v3.5 physical twin offer complementary strengths. Block 2 established the controller-level claim on bestest
air: the hybrid recipe (v3 dynamics + v3.5 disagreement reward-shaping) produces the strongest controller, with controller-family-specific λ values fixed at λ
temp = 0.10, λ
pwr = 5×10⁻⁵ for thermostatic PPO. **Block 3 asks: does this recipe transfer to BOPTEST testcases other than bestest
air?**
The Block 3 narrative follows the pre-registration / outcome / hypothesis closure structure:

§4 Transfer matrix — the headline result across testcase × regime × KPI.

§6 Secondary testcase (bestest_hydronic) — replication of the primary pattern.
§7 Stretch testcase (singlezone_commercial_hydronic) — pre-registered predictions and surprises.
§8 C_zon consistency analysis — the hydronic-family uniform-mass hypothesis.

§1.3  Claim boundary (manifest scope.deliberately_NOT_claimed)
Block 3 deliberately does NOT claim:
universal building generalisation
cross-climate generalisation (single weather file source)
transfer to fundamentally different HVAC topologies (e.g., VRF, radiant)
continual learning across multiple buildings without forgetting
The intended claim is narrower: 
the hybrid recipe transfers to closely related BOPTEST testcases under explicitly stated recalibration conditions and controller-interface compatibility
.
§1.4  Testcase selection (pre-registered)
Three single-zone hydronic testcases were pre-selected to span an increasing difficulty gradient relative to bestest_air. Selection criteria (fixed before inspecting any candidate response):
(a) single-zone or single-zone-like envelope
(b) heating-capable HVAC (cooling optional)
(c) actuator interface compatible with direct supply-temperature command OR overridable with documented mapping
(d) at least one structural difference from bestest_air (actuator, source, envelope size, or climate) so transfer is non-trivial

§2.  Actuator-interface adaptation
bestest
air exposes a **direct supply-temperature** actuation interface (the agent commands an air supply setpoint in 18–35 °C). The three hydronic testcases expose **different actuator types**: the heat pump testcase has a heat-pump setpoint-and-enable interface; the residential hydronic testcase has a boiler/radiator direct supply-setpoint interface; and the commercial hydronic testcase has a supply-valve interface. Without adaptation, the bestest
air-trained policy&apos;s action output would land on undefined BOPTEST actuator signals, and the comparison would not be a transfer test — it would be a programming error.
Each testcase gets a per-testcase 
actuator adapter config
 that maps the policy&apos;s continuous t_supply action onto the testcase&apos;s actuator interface. These configs are pre-registered separately (anchors eb7091e / 46fbaa9 / 645626e) so the mapping cannot be tuned post-hoc.
**bestest_hydronic_heat_pump**: adapter `hydronic_t_supply_to_setpoint_v1` (config: configs/block3_actuator_mapping_bestest_hydronic_heat_pump.yaml)
**bestest_hydronic**: adapter `hydronic_direct_supply_setpoint_adapter_v1`
**singlezone_commercial_hydronic**: adapter `commercial_hydronic_supply_valve_adapter_v1`

§10.  Synthesis and Block 4 implications
§10.1  What Block 3 establishes
**Surrogate-side transferability of v3.5 inverse calibration is supported on N=3** — Stage A/B/C produces 60–88% RMSE_T improvement on each new testcase, with consistent C_zon re-identification within a tight band.
**Hydronic-family uniform C_zon hypothesis is supported** (1.89–1.95× bestest_air, mean 1.92×, σ=0.032). The C_zon does not scale with zone volume in the tested range.
**Controller-side frozen transfer is falsified on N=3** — residential hydronic fails comfort; commercial stretch fails energy threshold (despite passing safety). Frozen-method transfer is not deployment-ready.
**Pre-registered claim boundary holds** — Block 3 deliberately did not claim universal building generalisation or continual learning. The negative controller-side result is exactly the kind of result the manifest was designed to surface honestly.
§10.2  Block 4 implications
The honest reading after Block 3 close points to four concrete future-work items, each falsifiable in its own right:
**Controller fine-tuning on target testcase**: Block 3 deliberately froze controllers. Block 4 can ask whether 100k-step BOPTEST live-finetune (as in Block 2 MORL §1.7) recovers comfort on the residential hydronic cases.
**Hybrid-family-specific λ values**: the bestest_air-optimal λ_temp = 0.10 may not be optimal for the hydronic actuator regime. A small λ sweep on the heat-pump testcase would parallel Block 2 §5 (HDRL sweep).
**Extended N for C_zon uniformity**: N=3 hydronic suggests uniform C_zon × 1.92, but a wider sample (heat-pump + boiler-radiator + valve + radiant + VRF) would establish whether the uniformity is hydronic-family or HVAC-family-wide.
**Energy-aware reward augmentation for the stretch regime**: the commercial testcase&apos;s +35% energy penalty suggests adding an energy term whose weight depends on the building scale, or a constraint-style energy ceiling, would shift the threshold-pass into a deployment-ready pass.
