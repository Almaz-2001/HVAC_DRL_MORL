  Keywords:                                                 In recent years, Transfer Learning (TL) has emerged as a promising solution to scale Deep Reinforcement Learning
  Transfer learning                                         (DRL) controllers for building energy management, addressing challenges related to DRL implementation as high
  Real implementation                                       data requirements and reliance on surrogate models. Moreover, most TL applications are limited to simulations,
  Deep reinforcement learning
                                                            not revealing their real performance in actual buildings. This paper explores the implementation of an online TL
  Building HVAC control
  Energy eﬃciency
                                                            methodology combining imitation learning and fine-tuning to transfer a DRL controller between two real oﬃce
                                                            environments.
                                                            Pre-trained in simulation using a calibrated digital twin, the DRL controller reduces energy consumption
                                                            and improves indoor temperature control when managing the operation of a Thermally Activated Building
                                                            System in one of the two oﬃces both in simulation and in the real field. Afterwards, the DRL controller is
                                                            transferred to the other oﬃce following the online TL methodology. The proposed approach outperforms a DRL
                                                            controller implemented without pre-training, and Rule-Based and Proportional-Integral controllers, achieving
                                                            energy savings between 6 and 40% and improving indoor temperature control between 30 and 50%. These
                                                            ﬁndings underscore the eﬃcacy of the online TL methodology as a viable solution to enhance the scalability of
                                                            DRL controllers in real buildings.
